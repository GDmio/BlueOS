BlueOS — A fan-made Android ROM inspired by Blue Archive

A fan-made Android ROM inspired by Blue Archive

Based on LineageOS 24.0 (Android 17), with Google Play built in. Free and open source, by GDmio.

DownloadSource code on GitHubTelegram channel

What it has

  • Its own logo, boot animation, fonts and sounds
  • A live halo over the lock-screen clock, Moe pixel clocks
  • Live widgets that look good together, with a music equalizer
  • «Colors»: every detail takes its colour from your wallpaper by its role
  • Live weather and an equalizer drawn right on the wallpaper
  • Arona or Plana live on the lock screen and in a widget: she reacts to touches, the phone's motion and notifications*

Game files

BlueOS contains no game files: no character models, voices, music or wallpapers from the game. The ROM works fully without them. You can add your own files right on the phone (Wallpaper & style → Character → Character files). Where to get them: the link in the description of the author's TikTok profile, tiktok.com/@chozadaun.

Questions

Is this vivo's BlueOS or Blue Robotics' BlueOS?

No. This BlueOS is a fan-made Android ROM by GDmio, inspired by the game Blue Archive. It only shares the name with those projects.

Is BlueOS official Blue Archive software?

No. It is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Nexon, Yostar or NEXON Games.

Does it have Google Play?

Yes, Google apps (MindTheGapps) are built in.

Is it free?

Yes. The ROM and its sources are free; see the licence in the repository.

Where are the news and new versions?

In the Telegram channel t.me/BlueOSfirmware and on the releases page.

⚠️ No warranty. Installing any custom ROM wipes the phone and needs an unlocked bootloader. Some apps may not work (tap to pay, some banking apps).
