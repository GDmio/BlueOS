A fan-made Android ROM inspired by Blue Archive

Everything here is made in the spirit of Blue Archive: a halo floats over the clock, the widgets are made to go together, and the colours follow your wallpaper on their own. Add the character files and Arona or Plana will move into your lock screen. Google Play comes built in.

Features

Everything that makes BlueOS feel like Blue Archive. Underneath it's regular LineageOS, so everything you're used to is still there.

schedule
Halo clockA halo hangs over the lock-screen clock and sways a little when you move the phone. There are Moe pixel clocks too, and you can change the size of the digits.
widgets
Matching widgetsMusic, weather, date, battery, quick toggles and more all look like one set, and they look good on any wallpaper.
palette
Colours from your wallpaperSet any wallpaper and the system picks colours to go with it. Don't like them? Choose your own for the clock, the halo and the text.
rainy
Weather on your wallpaperWhen it rains or snows outside, it rains or snows on your wallpaper too. Use your location or pick a city.
graphic_eq
An equalizer for your musicWhile music plays, bars jump, a wave runs or a glow pulses on your wallpaper and in a widget.
face
A living character*Arona or Plana live on your lock screen and in a widget. They react when you touch them, shake the phone or get a notification.

* The character needs files that aren't included in the ROM. See «Game files» below for how to add them.

Install

In short, it's three steps. The exact commands and everything you should know first are in your phone's guide.

1
Unlock the bootloaderThis erases everything on the phone, so back up what matters first.
2
Flash the BlueOS recoveryUse fastboot to flash boot, dtbo and vendor_boot from the release.
3
Install the ROMIn recovery choose Apply update → Apply from ADB and send the zip with adb sideload.
update
Updates keep your dataSince 0.8 a new version installs right over the old one. The phone is only wiped on the very first install.

Game files

folder_special
No game files includedBlueOS doesn't ship character models, voices, music or wallpapers from Blue Archive, and it works perfectly well without them. If you want a living Arona or Plana, add your own files right on the phone: Wallpaper & style → Character → Character files. The link to the files is in the description of the author's TikTok profile: tiktok.com/@chozadaun.
